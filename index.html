Tap Ball Rush - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 04 August 2026

Thank you for playing Tap Ball Rush.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles your information.

Information We Collect

Tap Ball Rush does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The game does not require:

  • Account registration
  • Login
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone

Local Game Data

The game stores your high score locally on your device only.

This information never leaves your device.

Internet Connection

Tap Ball Rush is fully playable offline.

Third-Party Services

This version of Tap Ball Rush does not use:

  • Advertising
  • Analytics
  • Tracking services
  • Cloud storage

Children's Privacy

Tap Ball Rush is suitable for players of all ages.

No personal information is collected from children or adults.

Changes

If future versions introduce online services or advertisements, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the Google Play Store listing.
