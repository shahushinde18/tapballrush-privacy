Tap Ball Rush - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 04 August 2026

Developer: Shahu Shinde

Thank you for playing Tap Ball Rush.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Tap Ball Rush handles your information.

Information We Collect

Tap Ball Rush does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The game does not require access to:

  • User accounts
  • Login credentials
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone

Local Game Data

Tap Ball Rush stores your high score locally on your device.

This information never leaves your device and is not shared with the developer or any third party.

Internet Connection

Tap Ball Rush is designed to work completely offline.

An internet connection is not required to play the game.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Tap Ball Rush does not use:

  • Advertisements
  • Analytics services
  • Tracking technologies
  • Cloud storage
  • User accounts

Children's Privacy

Tap Ball Rush is suitable for players of all ages.

No personal information is knowingly collected from children or adults.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions if new features such as advertisements, online services, analytics, or cloud functionality are introduced.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will always be available on this page.
