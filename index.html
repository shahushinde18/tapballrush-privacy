Tap Ball Rush - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 26 September 2026

Developer: Shahu Shinde

Thank you for playing Tap Ball Rush.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Tap Ball Rush handles your data and interacts with third-party services.

Information We Collect

Tap Ball Rush does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, or precise geolocation.

The game does not require access to:

  • User accounts
  • Login credentials
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone

Local Game Data

Tap Ball Rush stores gameplay preferences and your high score locally on your device storage. This information remains entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any external personal database or private server.

Third-Party Advertising & Network Access

Tap Ball Rush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner and interstitial ads). To deliver ads and prevent fraudulent traffic, the Google Mobile Ads SDK uses device network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) and may collect or process certain non-personal device information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • IP address (for general approximate location and diagnostics)
  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and screen size
  • Ad interaction metrics and diagnostic performance logs

Google’s handling of collected data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. For more details on how Google manages advertising information, please review: Google Ads Policy

Children's Privacy

Tap Ball Rush does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that non-compliant data has been collected, please contact us immediately so appropriate steps can be taken.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
